Zona Franca da Madeira (CINM) — IRC a 5%: licenciamento até 31.12.2026
Tax benefit · Companies · Deadline: 31/12/2026
Verified on 26/09/2026

Companies licensed in the International Business Centre of Madeira by 31 December 2026 pay 5% corporate tax (IRC) until 2033 on profits from operations with non-residents or other CINM entities. Job creation is required and, with 1 to 5 jobs, a minimum investment of €75,000 in the first two years.

Source: SDM · IBC Madeira — Read the official source

SSM REFUND
Social Mobility Subsidy (SSM)

Madeira residents get back part of the cost of flights between the island and the mainland or the Azores. On each claim you pay a co-payment of €79 (€59 for students).
